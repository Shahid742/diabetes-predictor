Understanding Our Diabetes Prediction System

How Does Our Prediction Model Work?

Our system is powered by machine learning algorithms trained on a dataset of over 100,000 patients. It takes into account several factors like:

  • Blood Glucose Levels
  • Insulin Levels
  • Body Mass Index (BMI)
  • Age, Genetics, and Lifestyle Factors

The model uses TensorFlow and is constantly updated to improve its accuracy. Once you input your details, the system calculates the probability of diabetes using statistical analysis and pattern recognition.

The Growing Threat of Diabetes

Diabetes is a global health crisis. Here are some alarming facts:

  • Over 537 million adults worldwide have diabetes.
  • 1 in 10 people is diabetic, and many remain undiagnosed.
  • Uncontrolled diabetes can lead to heart disease, kidney failure, blindness, and nerve damage.

Early detection and management are key to preventing complications. Our system helps users assess their risk and take early action.
