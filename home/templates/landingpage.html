    About Our Website

    Our diabetes prediction Website uses data from thousands of patients to accurately predict the risk of diabetes. By entering some basic information, you can get a prediction in seconds. Empowering individuals with AI-driven diabetes risk assessment.

    Who We Are

    Welcome to Diabetes Predictor, a cutting-edge web application designed to help users assess their risk of diabetes using advanced machine learning technology.

    Our Vision

    Diabetes is a global health concern affecting millions worldwide. Our vision is to empower individuals with knowledge and tools to take proactive steps in managing their health.

    How Our System Works

    • Users enter key health parameters such as age, BMI, glucose levels, and blood pressure.
    • Our AI-based predictive model, trained on over 100,000 real-world data samples, analyzes the input.
    • Within seconds, users receive a detailed risk assessment along with potential lifestyle recommendations.

    Why Choose Us?
